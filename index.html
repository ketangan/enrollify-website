Everything you need

Built for the whole enrollment flow.

From a first inquiry to a confirmed student — and the boring data-shuffle in between.

Custom enrollment forms

Every school gets a fully customized application form matched to your programs, fields, and branding. Configured by us, no coding required.

  • Waiver & consent fields signed at submission.
  • File uploads — medical forms, waivers, photos.
  • Embed on your site or open as a popup.

Organized submissions, instantly

Every application lands in a clean admin dashboard — sorted, searchable, filterable. No spreadsheets, no inbox digging, no copy-paste.

Save & resume + reminders

Families who start a form get a magic link to finish later. Automated reminders nudge them back — recovers leads you'd otherwise lose between the first inquiry and a confirmed enrollment.

Lead management

A built-in CRM to track prospective students from first inquiry to enrollment. Log calls, schedule follow-ups, manage your pipeline in one place.

  • One-click lead conversion — turn any prospect into a full application without re-entering data.

Reports that show what's working

See your conversion funnel, lead sources that actually convert, and where families drop off. No BI tools, no SQL, no spreadsheets to maintain.

  • Drop-off points across your funnel.
  • CSV export everywhere; Brightwheel and named export profiles on Pro+.
NEW

AI application summaries

Each submission gets an instant AI-generated summary, scored against your school's custom criteria. Review in seconds instead of minutes.
